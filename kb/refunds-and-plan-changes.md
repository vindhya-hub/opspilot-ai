# Refunds and Plan Changes

## Duplicate charges

Duplicate charges for the same subscription period are refunded in full to the original payment method as soon as both transactions are confirmed. Refunds usually appear on the card or bank statement within 5-10 business days, depending on the bank. A corrected invoice is issued automatically after the duplicate charge is refunded, so no separate request is needed.

## Accidental upgrades and downgrades

An accidental upgrade to a higher plan or to annual billing can be reversed within 14 days of the charge, and the price difference is refunded. After 14 days, plan changes take effect at the next renewal date and unused time is credited to the account instead of refunded. Downgrading never deletes data; features outside the new plan become read-only.

## Price changes at renewal

Price changes are announced by email at least 30 days before they apply to a renewal. If an account renewed at a higher price without that notice, the previous price is honoured for one more billing cycle and the difference is refunded. Renewal prices on annual contracts are locked for the full contract term.

## Refund limits

Refunds go to the original payment method only and cannot be split across cards. Unused days on monthly plans are not refunded, except where local law requires it or an SLA credit applies.
