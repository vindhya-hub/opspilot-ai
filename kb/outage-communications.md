# Incident and Outage Communications

## Declaring an incident

Reports from several customers, or from all users in one region, that the app is not loading or is failing are treated as a possible incident rather than as separate tickets. Support checks the status page and the incident channel first, then links new tickets to the open incident so that every affected customer receives the same updates.

## Updates during an outage

During an incident the status page shows what is affected and when the next update will be posted. Updates are posted at least every 30 minutes during a major incident, and they do not speculate about the root cause before it is confirmed. Workarounds are shared only after engineering has verified them.

## After the incident

A post-incident summary is published within five business days of a major incident. Business and Enterprise accounts whose monthly uptime falls below the 99.9% commitment receive a service credit automatically, without having to file a claim.
