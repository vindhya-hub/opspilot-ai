# Customer Communication and De-escalation

## Acknowledge the impact

Replies to frustrated customers start by acknowledging the specific impact on their work and apologising for it, without blaming the customer or another team. Every reply ends with a concrete next step and a time for the next update.

## Repeat contacts

Before replying to a follow-up, the full history of earlier tickets is read so the customer is never asked to repeat information they already provided. Customers who have contacted support several times about the same issue are offered a single named owner for the case.

## Cancellation, legal and public complaints

Mentions of cancelling a contract or involving a legal team are acknowledged calmly and passed to the account manager, without debating the contract in the reply. Public complaints on social media are answered publicly once with an invitation to continue in a private channel, and the case is then handled like any escalated ticket.
