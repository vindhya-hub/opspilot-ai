# Billing Disputes and Failed Payments

## Disputes and chargebacks

Payment disputes and chargebacks are handled by a billing specialist, who reviews the full charge history and offers a refund or credit decision on the same business day. Settling a disputed charge with us first is faster than a bank dispute, because an open bank dispute freezes billing changes on the account until it is closed. Disputed amounts are never deducted from future invoices without written agreement.

## Failed payments after a card update

Failed renewal payments are retried automatically after 24 hours, and again after three and five days. Updating the card under Billing > Payment methods triggers an immediate retry and clears the payment warning once the charge succeeds. Accounts keep full access during a 7-day grace period, after which they become read-only until the payment goes through.

## Invoice corrections and VAT

Invoices that include VAT even though a valid VAT ID is on file are reissued without VAT, and the overcharged amount is refunded or credited. VAT IDs are validated against the official registry, so the corrected invoice is issued as soon as the ID passes validation. Company name, address and purchase order details can be added to past invoices on request.
