# Data Exports and Privacy Requests

## Slow or stuck exports

Large exports such as audit logs, project archives or invoice histories can take up to six hours to prepare, and progress may show 0% until the first file is ready. An export that has made no progress after six hours is cancelled and restarted by support, which completes it in almost every case. A download link is emailed when the export is ready and stays valid for seven days.

## Incomplete or corrupted export files

Custom fields are included in CSV exports when the "Include custom fields" option is selected before the export starts. Export files that will not open are usually incomplete downloads, and downloading the file again from the export history page fixes most of them.

## GDPR and personal data deletion

Requests to delete personal data under GDPR are completed within 30 days and confirmed in writing to the requester. Deletion requests must come from an account admin, and the Privacy team verifies each request before any data is removed. Deleted personal data is purged from backups within a further 35 days.

## Full account exports

Account admins can request a full export of all records and attachments, which is prepared within two business days. Full exports for contract reviews or migrations are delivered as an encrypted archive with a checksum.
