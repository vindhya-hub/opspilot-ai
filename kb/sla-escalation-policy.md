# SLA and Escalation Policy

## SLA windows

Every ticket must be resolved, or have an action plan agreed with the customer, within the SLA window for the customer's tier: 72 hours for Free, 24 hours for Pro, 8 hours for Business and 4 hours for Enterprise accounts. The SLA clock starts when the ticket is created and pauses only while support is waiting for a reply from the customer.

## Mandatory escalation triggers

A ticket is escalated to a senior specialist when a Business or Enterprise ticket has breached its SLA window, when the customer has contacted support three or more times in 30 days about the same issue, or when the customer mentions cancelling, legal action, a chargeback or a public complaint. Suspected security incidents and data loss are always escalated, whatever the tier.

## How escalation works

An escalated ticket gets a named owner within one hour, and the customer is told who owns the case and when they will hear back next. Enterprise escalations also notify the account manager. The owner updates the customer at least once per SLA window until the issue is resolved.

## Requests for a manager

A request to speak with a manager is acknowledged within one hour, and a team lead takes ownership of the ticket. The team lead reads the full ticket history before replying, so the customer never has to repeat information.
